Ways To Earn - Maelezo Kamili

💻 1. Freelancing

Ni nini? Jifunze skill kama design, writing, video editing na uuize kwa wateja online.

Unahitaji: Simu / Laptop + Internet + Skill moja

Unapata: Ksh 500 - Ksh 5,000 kwa kazi moja

Jinsi ya Kuanza: 1. Chagua skill moja (Canva design ni rahisi) 2. Jifunze YouTube siku 7 3. Fungua account Fiverr / Upwork 4. Posta kazi zako

📱 2. Content Creation

Ni nini? TikTok, YouTube, Instagram - tengeneza content na brands zitakulipa.

Unahitaji: Simu + Mtandao + Kuwa consistent

Unapata: Ksh 1,000 - Ksh 50,000 kwa mwe
